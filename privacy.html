Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

AttachmentDownloader (the "app", at attachmentdownloader.ca) helps you find attachments in your Gmail and download them as a ZIP. This page explains what it accesses and keeps.

What the app accesses

Google account sign-in and Gmail access are separate:

  • Your basic profile: name, email address and profile picture, used to sign you in.
  • Read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly), granted directly to your browser when you connect Gmail. Google classifies this permission as able to view Gmail messages and settings; it is required to search messages and retrieve their attachments. The app cannot send, change or delete email.

The app can't send, change or delete any email.

What stays on your device

  • Your short-lived Gmail access token, held in browser memory while connected and cleared when you disconnect or close the page.
  • Your search query and matching attachment details, held in browser memory while the page is open.
  • Attachment bytes and the ZIP file, processed in your browser.

Gmail search requests, attachment details and file contents are sent directly between your browser and Google. They are not sent to Convex or our website server. Search results are cleared when you leave or reload the app.

What our backend stores

Convex stores your basic Google profile (name, email and picture) for account sign-in. It does not receive your Gmail access token, Gmail search query, message details or attachment files. Older Gmail connections from before this change are revoked and their saved search results deleted when you next open the app.

Who processes your data

The account backend runs on Convex; the website is hosted on Vercel. Google processes Gmail API requests. The app uses no advertising or analytics trackers and doesn't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show you your attachments and download them. It isn't used for advertising, isn't read by people, and isn't used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Click Disconnect Gmail in the app. This revokes the browser's Gmail grant and clears the token and search results from this device.
  • You can also remove the app at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections.
  • Click Delete account in the app to revoke Gmail access and permanently delete your account and sign-in records from our backend.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: open an issue on GitHub.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too.
